# MowGo Social Media Strategy

## Platforms

| Platform | Content Type | Frequency | Growth Driver |
|----------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| Facebook Page | Text posts, link shares, tips | 1-2x/day | Organic reach + your ads |
| Instagram Business | Photos, carousels | 1x/day | Hashtags, carousel reach |

---

## Target Audience

Lawn care business owners running 1-5 crews. Age 25-55. On their phone between jobs. They want to make more money with less stress. They don't want "marketing" — they want straight talk from someone who gets it.

---

## Voice

Direct. No corporate fluff. No grass puns. Write like the one guy in the crew who figured out the business side and wants to help others do the same.

- ✅ "Stop texting clients at 10pm. Here's what to do instead."
- ❌ "Optimize your customer communication workflow with MowGo's innovative platform!"

---

## Content Pillars

### 1. Business Tips (40% of posts)

The main hook. Actionable advice that makes owners feel seen.

Examples:
- "How to price a new lawn so you actually profit (formula inside)"
- "When to fire a bad customer — and why it's worth it every time"
- "You're not a lawn care company. You're a logistics company that cuts grass."
- "The math on hiring your first crew vs. staying solo"
- "What I learned from my first $100k year in lawn care"

### 2. Efficiency / Automation (25% of posts)

Soft MowGo positioning. Show the problem, hint at the solution.

Examples:
- "If you're still using a whiteboard to schedule, read this"
- "3 things top lawn care owners do every Sunday night"
- "How much is a missed appointment actually costing you?"
- "Route optimization isn't optional anymore — here's the math"
- "The hidden cost of 'just one more client' without systems"

### 3. Industry Relatability (20% of posts)

Engagement bait. Gets comments, shares, algorithm love.

Examples:
- "What's the one tool you'd never switch brands on?"
- "Hot take: leaf cleanup should be billed separately. Fight me."
- "Client asked for 'just a quick trim.' It was a jungle. Send help."
- "Fill in the blank: the hardest part of lawn care is ___"
- "POV: it's 95 degrees and your mower won't start"

### 4. Seasonal / Timely (10% of posts)

Relevant content that rides the calendar.

- Spring: Booking rush, onboarding new clients, equipment prep
- Summer: Heat safety, upselling, maintenance schedules
- Fall: Leaf cleanup, aeration, pre-pay annual discounts
- Winter: Equipment maintenance, planning, "book now for spring pricing"

### 5. MowGo Product (5% of posts)

Only 1 in 20 posts. Nobody follows a page that's all ads.

Examples:
- "How one owner cut scheduling time by 10 hours/week"
- Feature spotlight disguised as a tip ("Here's a quick way to...")
- Free trial call-to-action (sparingly)

---

## Posting Schedule (Cron Automation)

### Facebook — Daily at 9 AM EST
- Mix of tips, relatability, efficiency content
- Rotate pillars so no two same-type posts in a row
- Seasonal posts on relevant dates

### Instagram — Daily at 12 PM EST
- Photo posts with branded lawn care imagery
- Carousels (2-5 slides) for tip/guide content (better reach)
- 15-20 relevant hashtags per post
- Caption is the same text as Facebook post (repurposed)

### Weekly rhythm:
| Day | Facebook | Instagram |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| Mon | Business tip | Tip carousel |
| Tue | Efficiency | Photo + tip |
| Wed | Relatability / question | Engagement post |
| Thu | Business tip | Tip carousel |
| Fri | Efficiency | Photo + tip |
| Sat | Seasonal / light | Seasonal photo |
| Sun | Relatability / question | Rest |
