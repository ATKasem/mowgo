## 3. What this means for your numbers

Quick math for a 1/3-acre yard, the OK median, at the state average:

- **Per cut:** $52.49 (bi-weekly) → **~$1,365/yr per customer** (26 cuts)
- **20 customers** = ~$27,300/yr in mowing alone
- **30 customers** = ~$41,000/yr

Add the extras OK customers already buy — fertilization (9%), cleanup (8%), leaf removal (8%), aeration (8%) — and the average customer's annual value climbs 30%+ above mowing alone.

## 4. The honest caveats (read this before changing prices)

1. **Your market sets the ceiling, your work sets the price.** These are averages of what OK customers actually pay — not a guarantee you can charge it. Premium service (reliable scheduling, reminders, receipts) justifies the top of the range; inconsistent service gets the bottom.
2. **Yard size dominates.** A 1-acre cut pays 2x a 1/4-acre cut. Measure your accounts, don't guess.
3. **Raise on new customers first.** Existing customers notice price changes; new ones have nothing to compare against. When you raise, raise for new bookings, grandfather your loyal ones, and announce it before the season.
4. **One missed job costs more than any rate raise.** At these prices, a single lost weekly customer is ~$1,365/yr. Reliability (showing up, even after rain) is worth more than a $5 rate bump.
