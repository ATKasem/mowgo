## MONDAY: Business Tip (FB text + IG 2-slide carousel)

**Hormozi:** §9 pain is the pitch, sell at deprivation. §41 numbers make it real.

**Hook:** "Your worst client costs you more than your best one makes you."

**Body:**
The bottom 10% of your client list eats more time than the top 10% pays for. You know them:
they pay late, they renegotiate after the work is done, they text you at 9pm about the
one blade of grass you missed.

Here is the math. One late-paying client costs you 3 extra touches a month. Text, reminder,
re-send. Ten minutes each. Thirty minutes a month on one client. At $45 an hour that is
$22 a month just to collect what they owe. For a $39 lawn. That is not a client, that is a
hobby with extra steps.

The fix is not raising prices. It is firing the bottom 10% and raising prices for everyone
else. One fewer bad client pays for itself in peace of mind.

**CTA:** "Who was your worst client ever? Drop it in the comments."

## TUESDAY: Efficiency (FB text + IG photo)

**Hormozi:** §44 Rule of 100 (time leverage). §13 name the enemy (the enemy is Monday morning).

**Hook:** "Rain day math: 8 clients, 45 minutes of text-back-and-forth."

**Body:**
It rains Tuesday. By 7am you have 8 texts: "Are you still coming?" "Can you do Thursday?"
"I need it before Saturday." You spend 45 minutes doing receptionist work for free. Every
rainy week, 45 minutes. At $45 an hour, that is $34 a week of your time, gone, every time
it rains.

You are not in the business of texting people back. You are in the business of mowing.
The difference between a crew owner and a crew that works for themselves is whether the
rain day moves 8 jobs with one action or with 32 texts.

**CTA:** "How long does your rain-day ritual take? Be honest."

## THURSDAY: Business Tip (FB text + IG 2-slide carousel)

**Hormozi:** §9 sell at deprivation (show what they pay now). §41 the math is the proof.

**Hook:** "$29 per user. Per crew member. Every month. Forever."

**Body:**
If you run a 3-person crew on the big-name software, you are not paying $139 a month.
You are paying $139 for the base, then $29 per month per extra user. That is $197 a month
for a 3-person operation. $2,364 a year. For a business that does maybe 40-60 lawns a week
in season.

The industry built its pricing for 20-person companies with office managers. A 1-3 person
crew does not need 20-person pricing. Flat price per crew, not per head, is the entire
argument.

**CTA:** "What does your software cost per crew member right now? Do the math in the comments."

## FRIDAY: Efficiency (FB text + IG photo)

**Hormozi:** §44 leverage, remove friction. Soft MowGo (describe the flow, not the pitch).

**Hook:** "You are 20 feet from the mailbox. Your invoice is already sent."

**Body:**
The difference between getting paid in 3 days and getting paid in 3 weeks is usually one
thing: when the invoice goes out. Invoice at the curb, before you start the truck, and the
client pays before the week's work piles up on top.

The flow that works: finish the job, tap done, invoice sends itself, client pays from the
link in the text, you see paid vs unpaid when you get back to the shop. Ten seconds of work
replaces two days of chasing.

**CTA:** "Do you invoice same-day or end-of-week? Why?"

## SATURDAY: Seasonal (FB text + IG photo)

**Hormozi:** §26 timeliness beats evergreen. Be the expert, earn the follow.

**Hook:** "August heat rule: mow higher."

**Body:**
Bermuda wants 2 to 3 inches in August. Cool-season grass wants 3.5 to 4. Scalping in 95
degree heat stresses the turf and shows up as brown patches in September that clients will
blame you for.

Two cheap wins this month: raise the deck one notch, and sharpen the blades every 10 hours
of cutting. Dull blades tear, and torn grass burns in heat. Sharp blades are free marketing.

**CTA:** "What is your August mow height? Tell us your mix."
