What is rain delay software?

Rain delay software is lawn care scheduling software that automatically handles your route when weather forces you to reschedule. Instead of opening each client record and moving every job by hand — then texting each client — a rain delay button shifts your entire day to the next available day in one tap, and notifies clients automatically.
