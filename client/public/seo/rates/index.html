What this means for your numbers

Quick math for a 1/3-acre yard at the state average:

  • Per cut: $52.49 (bi-weekly) → ~$1,365/yr per customer (26 cuts)
  • 20 customers = ~$27,300/yr in mowing alone
  • 30 customers = ~$41,000/yr

Add the extras OK customers already buy — fertilization (9%), cleanup (8%), leaf removal (8%), aeration (8%) — and the average customer's annual value climbs 30%+ above mowing alone.

The honest caveats

1. Your market sets the ceiling, your work sets the price. Averages are what OK customers pay, not a guarantee. Premium service (reliable scheduling, reminders, receipts) justifies the top of the range.

2. Yard size dominates. A 1-acre cut pays 2x a 1/4-acre cut. Measure your accounts, don't guess.

3. Raise on new customers first. Grandfather loyal ones, announce it before the season.

4. One missed job costs more than any rate raise. A lost weekly customer is ~$1,365/yr. Showing up — even after rain — is worth more than a $5 bump.
