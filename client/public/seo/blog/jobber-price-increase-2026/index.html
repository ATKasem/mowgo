Why It Matters for Small Crews

For a solo operator or 1–3 person crew, each added user raises the bill. Here is the math:

  • 1A solo operator on Jobber Connect pays $139/month for features they might not need (advanced reporting and team management). You're paying for a business you don't have yet.
  • 2A 3-person crew on Jobber Connect pays a $139 base plus $29/month for each of two additional users, totaling $197/month. For a lawn care business earning $8–12K per month, that is a meaningful software expense.
  • 3Grow plan at $349/mo: That's over $4,000/year on software. Most small crews don't need advanced reporting dashboards — they need to know where to mow next and whether it's going to rain.

If software costs squeeze your small crew, compare the monthly total before you renew. You have options.
