What are per-user fees in lawn care software?

Per-user fees mean you pay a separate monthly charge for every crew member who uses the software. For example, Jobber charges $29/user/mo on top of the base plan. A 2-person crew pays $168/mo total on Jobber Connect, while MowGo charges $39/mo flat for the entire crew.

Does MowGo really have no per-user fees?

Correct. MowGo Solo is $39/mo flat. There are no per-user fees, no add-on charges, and no hidden costs. Your whole crew uses it for one price.

Why do other lawn care apps charge per user?

Per-user pricing is a common SaaS model that scales revenue with team size. It works well for enterprise tools but penalizes small crews. MowGo was built specifically for 1-3 person operations, so flat pricing makes more sense.
