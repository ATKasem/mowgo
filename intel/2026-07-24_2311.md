## 1. COMPETITOR DEEP DIVE: YARDBOOK

### Current Pricing (Verified July 2026)
Yardbook now has THREE tiers, not just Free/Premium:
- **Free**: Scheduling, invoicing, CRM, routing, time tracking, estimates. Shows in-app ads.
- **Business: $34.99/mo** — adds bulk SMS/email, GPS tracking, invoice reminders
- **Enterprise: $49.99/mo** — adds branded app, QuickBooks sync, multi-step chemical programs

Previously reported as $25/mo Premium — this was outdated. The $34.99/$49.99 pricing was confirmed July 2025 (Connecteam review) and remains current.

### Key Weaknesses Confirmed/Updated:
1. **GPS syncs every 4 hours** — confirmed by Connecteam review. "Makes real-time tracking impractical."
2. **No iOS app** — iOS is in beta. Android only for real mobile use.
3. **No break/overtime tracking** — compliance risk for states like California.
4. **Dated UI** — "hasn't been meaningfully redesigned in years" (DoorstepHQ blog), "looks outdated" (Connecteam), "functional but older, busier look" (FieldCompare).
5. **Invoice deliverability issues** — "routinely report issues sending invoices that end up in customers' spam folders" (fieldservicesoftware.io).
6. **No job notifications** — employees must constantly check app; no push notifications for new assignments.
7. **Credit card processing: ~3.9%** — Stripe 2.9% + $0.30 + 1% Yardbook fee on free plan.
8. **Support: email-only, 24-48hr response** — no live chat, no phone support.
9. **No Kanban/progress tracking** — only color-coding for job status.
10. **Recurring invoices require manual clicks** — not automated.

### User Verbatims:
- "Very very basic" — LawnSite user looking at Jobber instead
- "The interface is dated and hasn't been meaningfully redesigned in years" — DoorstepHQ comparison
- "Invoice deliverability is the biggest practical problem" — fieldservicesoftware.io
- "No iOS app. This is a dealbreaker for many operators" — LawnCrewPro

## 3. MARKET GAPS

### LawnSite 2026 Software Thread — Gold Mine
**User: prezek** — 1000+ customers, 4-5 mowing crews, $1.5-1.8M revenue, STILL using Excel spreadsheets.

**Verbatim pain points:**
> "What I don't want is something I need to click 100 different customers to juggle things around due to weather/holiday/call out/whatever. In excel I can cut and paste blocks of customers to juggle things since it's all in front of me-in a matter of minutes."

> "Route optimization still sucks, because everyone is basing it on Googlewhatever and that seems to be the problem."

> "Route optimization will never ever be what you want it to be. For all softwares."

**Key insight**: Even at $1.5M+ revenue, operators use Excel because NO software handles weather rescheduling as well as "cut and paste blocks of customers." This is MowFlow's EXACT differentiator — rain delay auto-reschedule is the solution to this specific pain.

### Other Gaps Found:
- **Route optimization universally criticized** — "will never ever be what you want it to be" (LawnSite consensus)
- **Weather-based rescheduling** — "if ur crew gets rained out and they have half the jobs left they can choose to skip those jobs due to rain" — this is manual, not automatic (Service Autopilot)
- **"I wish someone could program routing/optimization as efficiently as I can by just looking at a map"** — Mark Oomkes, LawnSite
- **Chemical tracking gaps** — Jobber doesn't have it; SAP "sucks for chemicals"
- **No good mid-tier option** between free (Yardbook) and expensive (Jobber $119+)
