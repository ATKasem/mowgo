## 1. Market: mild-moderate growth, recurring maintenance is the core
- US lawn care services: **$60.0B (2025) → $62.91B (2026) → $79.68B by 2031** (Mordor, 4.85% CAGR). Landscaping services $176.7B, +0.9% in 2026 (IBISWorld).

## 2. Software adoption is THE 2026 story — and the switch triggers are ours
- Commercial landscapers: **62% use 7+ software systems, 28% use 10+** (NALP/Aspire, Feb 2026). Most-used: accounting 72%, invoicing 67%, estimating 65%, marketing automation 62%, payroll 62%, GPS 58%, end-to-end mgmt 55%.
- **Top reasons for switching: automate workflows (58%), operational efficiency (51%), address feature gaps (44%)** — exactly MowGo's auto-invoice + merged route + one-tap day view.
- Granum/LMN 2026 digital adoption report (n≈700): **49.4% say training/implementation is the biggest tech adoption challenge**; fully-digitized operators are widening margins ("digital core" gap).
- Landscaping software market: **11.7% CAGR 2026-2033** — software growing ~2.5x faster than the industry it serves.

## 3. Consumer: more money, less DIY

- → Homeowners are spending more and outsourcing more: pricing confidence for pros = MowGo's $49-79 stays a rounding error on a customer's annual lawn budget.

- → One-tap route merge, stop-by-stop day view, "mark done" speed = direct labor-productivity story for the 1-3 person crew.
