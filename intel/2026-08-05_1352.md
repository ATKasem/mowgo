## Recommended features (new this run, ranked: impact × evidence / effort)

### 1. ⭐ Client booking link (self-booking page) — the gated-everywhere wedge
- **Evidence:** QuoteIQ gates InstaSchedule/InstaQuote (customer self-booking + self-quoting) behind **Elite $299 / Max $699** — "lower tiers require manual scheduling" (their own words). Jobber's Client Hub (view quotes, approve, pay, schedule) lives at **Connect $119+**. GorillaDesk ships a customer portal but starts $49 with per-route pricing. Every competitor in the $39-79 band gates it or doesn't have it.
- **Fit:** MowGo already auto-creates jobs + auto-invoices; a single public booking link (client picks an open slot from the owner's route → creates a job) is the exact "they gate it at $299, we include it at $39" line the compare page needs. **Effort: M (one public page + slot logic). Impact: High (differentiator + fills cancellations).** Reuses Stripe/supabase infra already built.

### 2. ⭐ Missed-call text-back — Jobber's AI Receptionist, the $0-AI version
- **Evidence:** Jobber AI Receptionist (GA Aug 2025, 200,000+ conversations processed per fieldcamp.ai) texts back callers who hang up before connecting. r/smallbusiness thread `1vfyelq` (draft #44) shows owners are now *receiving* AI assistant calls — the missed-call gap is live pain on both sides of the phone.
- **Fit:** MowGo doesn't need an AI bot. Twilio missed-call webhook → auto-text "sorry we missed you, reply here to book." The Twilio number is ALREADY being onboarded for rain-delay SMS (spec 08-05) — near-zero marginal cost, one more edge function. **Effort: S. Impact: Medium-High (leads that otherwise vanish).**

### 3. Auto quote follow-up nudge (day-3) — complete the loop
- **Evidence:** Jobber markets "automatically send email or text reminders about open quotes"; QuoteIQ AI Autopilot runs follow-up sequences after every quote sent. MowGo's day-7 unpaid-invoice nudge exists (the Blasian hook); the quote side of the loop is missing.
- **Fit:** Same nudge engine, different trigger: quote sent → no reply in 3 days → auto text. **Effort: S (reuses nudge cron). Impact: Medium (more booked jobs per quote).** Symmetric with the existing hook story.

### 4. Price-book quoting from stored yard size ($/1000 sq ft)
- **Evidence:** QuoteIQ's headline claim: MapMeasure Pro aerial sq-ft measurement = "the single biggest margin shift available to a lawn care operator in 2026"; Jobber and GorillaDesk have NO native property measurement (per QuoteIQ's own comparison — biased source, treat as directional). Yardbook has basic lot measurement. MowGo job cards ALREADY store yard size.
- **Fit:** Skip the aerial tracing (M, watch list). Ship the cheap half: per-client sq ft + service price book → 3-tap quote. **Effort: S-M. Impact: Medium (quoting speed + consistency).** Aerial tracing stays on watch list.

### 5. Profit visibility (paid − expenses, per month)
- **Evidence:** r/lawncare_canada `1rjwqtt` dev thread: lawn pros track "everything in their head, a notes app, or paper... no idea if the business is actually profitable." Jobber ships profit alerts. Pain confirmed in OK_LEADS_100 notes (cash-flow blindness).
- **Fit:** Simple expenses line + "this month: invoiced / paid / outstanding / net" card in Invoices tab. **Effort: S-M. Impact: Medium (retention — becomes the owner's scoreboard).**
