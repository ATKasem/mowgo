# MowGo BI Intel — Pricing Intelligence (Aug 15, 2026)

## Lane: pricing_intelligence (rotation index 2/4 → next: feature_ideas)

## Key Findings

### GreenRoute — Aggressive Free Plan ($0), Route Planning at $10/mo Flat

GreenRoute's Starter plan is genuinely free with unlimited customers, contracts, invoicing, credit card payments, satellite property measurement, web dashboard + mobile app. Professional adds drive route planning, weather forecasts, SMS, payment reminders, unlimited photos for $10/month flat. No per-user fees ever. Professional Plus at $50 has teams, expense tracking, P&L reports. Enterprise at $199.

**Why this matters:** This directly competes with MowGo Free tier at solo level and Solo $39 at pro features. $10 for route planning undercuts all competitors. The 90-day free trial on all pro features is aggressive. A 1-3 person crew paying $50 for Pro Plus beats MowGo Crew $79 hands down. Not a big threat yet (small brand, no SEO presence), but worth monitoring.

**MowGo response:** Route planning at $39/mo for Crew plan remains compelling if bundled well. GreenRoute's feature depth (P&L reports, geotag overlays, service report PDFs) is solid but they're a small player with no significant community presence.

### Shifton — Free Forever Up to 10 Employees

Shifton offers free forever with up to 10 team members, full shift scheduling, time clock & attendance, mobile app. Additional modules are per-employee: payroll $0.50/emp/mo, time off $1/emp/mo, work location tracking $5/emp/mo. Annual billing saves 20%.

**Why this matters:** For 1-3 person lawn crews, Shifton is effectively free. However, it's NOT lawn-specific — no invoicing, no estimates, no customer CRM, no satellite measurement. It's pure workforce scheduling/time tracking. Indirect competitor only for the smallest operators who might not need FSM features yet.

**MowGo positioning:** MowGo's value prop (estimate → schedule → invoice → collect) is fundamentally different from Shifton's crew management angle. Good to note for when we encounter contractors comparing options.

### Jobber Pricing — Confirmed Trap Narrative

Jobber's Core plan now lists $49/month monthly or $29/month annual (up from prior $24 promo). But the real damage is at Connect ($149/$99 annual) and above. Per-user fees at $29/user add up fast. YouTube video "Jobber's Pricing IS A TRAP" (17:30 min, posted Spring 2026) has significant engagement. Multiple comparison articles confirming the hidden-cost narrative.

**Actual costs confirmed:**
- Core: $49/mo (1 user) → cheapest entry point
- Connect: $149/mo (5 users) = $29.80/user
- Grow: $249/mo (10 users) = $24.90/user  
- Plus: $399/mo (15 users) = $26.60/user

With reviews add-on ($39 extra), referrals ($29 extra), AI Receptionist (+$50?), a Connect plan easily hits $250+/mo.

**Why this matters:** The anti-Jobber sentiment is organic and growing. Every thread asking about software mentions Jobber first, then complaints about pricing complexity follow in replies. MowGo's simple $39/$79/$199 flat-rate model is a clear advantage here.

### Housecall Pro — Pricing Stable, Per-User Overage Hits Hard

Basic: $79/mo ($59 annual) — 1 user only. Essentials: $189/mo ($149 annual) — 5 users. MAX: $329/mo ($299 annual) — 8 users, then $35/extra user. No longer has a free tier (eliminated it).

**Comparison with MowGo:** HCP Basic at $79 is double MowGo Solo at $39. At HCP Essentials level ($149 for 5 users), that's 3x MowGo Solo price for just 5 users where MowGo would cover unlimited. The gap widens dramatically at scale.

### Yardbook — Free Ad-Supported Tier Holds Steady

Yardbook's Starter plan remains truly free ($0/month, ad-supported). Charges 1% transaction fee on payments. Paid tiers not publicly listed but estimated around $35/month to remove transaction fee. No per-user fees. Limited routing, no offline mode on free tier.

**Competitive position:** Yardbook competes head-to-head with MowGo Free tier. Advantage for Yardbook: more established brand, better UI polish. Disadvantage: ad-supported on free tier (annoying for professional use).

### Market Context — Rising Prices Across the Board

Multiple sources confirm 2026 trend of increasing software prices across lawn care FSM:
- Jobber raised from $24→$29 (Core annual)
- Housecall Pro eliminated free tier entirely
- Most competitors adding per-user fees where previously flat

This creates headwinds for MowGo's free-tier strategy but also opens opportunities as contractors get sticker shock from their current provider.
