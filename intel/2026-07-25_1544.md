- Starter: $0/mo (unlimited customers, contracts, invoices, scheduling, satellite measurement)
- Professional: $10/mo (route planning, weather forecasts, service/payment reminders, customer loss alerts, SMS, reports)

4. Route optimization locked at $10/mo (Professional) — not on free tier
5. SMS notifications only on paid tiers (free tier has basic email only)
6. QuickBooks only on Enterprise ($199/mo) — highest tier only
7. NO AI Autopilot at any tier
8. Team management only at Professional Plus ($50/mo+)
9. Payment/service reminders only at $10/mo (Professional+)
10. Customer portal available on ALL tiers (corrects earlier intel — NOT locked behind $199)
11. Before/after photo pairing only at Pro Plus ($50/mo)
12. Logo customization only at Pro Plus ($50/mo)
13. 5 photo limit on free tier
14. No multi-crew support below $50/mo

- Pro: $99/mo first route (same as before)
- Growth: $149/mo first route (WAS $137/mo) — **INCREASED**

- Add-ons: SMS texting $5/mo for local number + bulk text packages
