## 2. New entrant watch: ProBase FREE (LawnStarter-backed)

LawnStarter's ProBase launched as a **completely free** lawn care software for 1-5 crews (50-200+ stops/week). Revenue model: job lead marketplace (takes a cut when you claim a lead). Features: invoicing, drag-and-drop scheduling, payment processing, route optimization, per-crew job views, AI service notes, tip collection.

**Read:** ProBase is the most serious free entrant — it's not ad-supported or crippled-free; it's genuinely functional. But the catch is it funnels users toward LawnStarter job leads (their monetization). MowGo's independent-operator positioning ($39 flat, no marketplace cut, no middleman) is the clean counter.

**Watch for:** ProBase gaining traction in "Top free software" lists (already #1 on LawnStarter's own comparison). Their free + marketplace model targets the same solo/small-crew segment MowGo targets, but from the lead-generation direction rather than the tooling direction.

**The free tier war is real.** Lawn care operators have 4+ free options before they reach MowGo. But MowGo has two structural advantages:
1. **No per-user tax** — Crew $79 covers unlimited techs vs competitors charging per-seat (LawnPro $249/15u, RazorSync $439/15u)
2. **No marketplace lock-in** — ProBase/LawnStarter free users pay via lead-gen margins; MowGo free (5 clients) is genuinely no-strings
3. **Rain delay + booking link** — none of the free tiers have both (ProBase has no weather/rain feature; LawnBook FREE has no web dashboard; LawnPro Free has no route optimization)
