## MowGo read

- **Threat: LOW–MODERATE, and not a pure SaaS competitor.** MX Build is a payments-first construction-finance play by a NASDAQ payment processor; the $49 is likely a lead-gen hook for processing + card revenue, not a flat SaaS sticker. Their depth (compliance, lien waivers, card programs, AP automation) overshoots the 1–3 crew lawn ICP; they lack lawn-specific scheduling/routing focus.
- **Do NOT chase their pricing.** MowGo can't (and shouldn't) match a payments-subsidized $49. Keep the honest-sticker line: MowGo $39 flat, month-to-month, no signup fee, no processing kicker.
- **Compare-page stance:** no SA/… — MX Build does NOT earn a compare-page column or note. If a lead mentions it, the truthful line is: "no public pricing, sales-call model, built for contractors/GCs, payments-first — not built for recurring lawn routes."
- **Watch triggers:** lawn-specific case studies, lawn comparison tables, route/recurring scheduling features, or lawn FB ad scale-up → signals a real vertical push into MowGo territory. Also monitor whether the $49 appears on a public pricing page.
