### Jobber Weakness Catalog (13 verified weaknesses)

1. **No inventory management** — cannot track parts or materials at all. "Insane in 2026" — Toricent Labs. Confirmed by FieldPickr.
2. **QuickBooks sync "atrocious"** — users removing sync and manually entering items. Verbatim: "The sync between QBO and Jobber is atrocious. We ended up removing the sync and manually entering those items."
3. **Reporting & Analytics: 5/10** — "the weakest link, no custom reports" (FieldCamp)
4. **Route Optimization: 6/10** — "improved in late 2025, still basic" (FieldCamp)
5. **Integrations: 6/10** — QuickBooks sync remains unreliable
6. **No chemical/application tracking** built-in (Aimadefor — GorillaDesk beats them here)
7. **Core tier missing critical features:** no time tracking, no expense tracking, no QuickBooks sync
8. **Two-way texting locked behind Grow** ($199+/mo month-to-month)
9. **Job costing locked behind Plus** ($499+/mo)
10. **Per-user pricing punishes growth:** "The per-user pricing model can quietly push your monthly bill past $600" (FieldCamp). 20+ employees = prohibitive (CostBench)
11. **Mobile app requires active internet** — no offline mode. Progressive web app, not native.
12. **"Key features feel locked behind higher-tier plans or add-ons"** — Nikole R., Capterra reviewer
13. **Real-world cost for 10-person team:** ~$20,728/year (Toricent Labs analysis)

## NEW COMPETITORS DISCOVERED

### FieldFuze Pro ($349/mo flat, 15 seats)
- Direct Jobber outgrower alternative
- Same $349/mo as Jobber Grow but gives 15 seats instead of 1
- Card processing 2.2% vs Jobber's 2.9%+$0.30
- Includes inventory management, insurance claims tracking, auto time tracking, commission tracking
- No per-user fees
- Has AI assistant
- Focuses on HVAC, plumbing, electrical — not lawn-care-specific

### CrewNest (flat pricing, free plan)
- Jobber alternative with "flat pricing and free plan instead of Jobber's $29/mo per added user"
- No details on lawn care specialization

### FieldZenPro (offline-first mobile, $?/mo)
- Key differentiator: offline-first native iOS/Android apps (Jobber requires internet)
- Built-in payroll processing, GPS fleet tracking, inventory management
- Free 48-hour migration from Jobber
- Targets 8-10+ technician businesses outgrowing Jobber

### GorillaDesk ($45/mo starting)
- Route-based recurring services (pest/lawn/pressure-washing)
- Only tool of top 3 with native chemical/application tracking built in
- Single-seat tiers only (no multi-user plans)
- $45-137/mo billed annually
- Directly competes on lawn care chemical tracking (MowFlow also has this)
