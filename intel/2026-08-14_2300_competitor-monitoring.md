# MowGo BI Intel — Competitor Monitoring (Aug 14, 2026)

## Key Findings

### Jobber
- Pricing stable since Q2. Core $29/mo anchor confirmed permanent after expired $24 promo.
- No new feature announcements. AI features (Voice AI, Auto-Draft) are lagging behind QuoteIQ's pipeline.
- Organic anti-Jobber sentiment growing on Reddit/YouTube. Independent creator video: "Jobber's Pricing IS A TRAP"
- First quarterly price increase in recent memory ($24→$29). Bad optics for them.

### Housecall Pro
- May 2026 product updates shipped: rebuilt mobile app, automated sales tax, upgraded routing.
- Pricing unchanged at $59/$149/$299 annual tiers. Still the steepest per-user jump.
- AI marketing push ("AI team members") but no pricing changes to match.

### QuoteIQ
- June 2026: 60+ features including AI automations, estimate templates, online booking.
- July 2026: AI AutoMeasure (satellite), Ask AI Reports, Expenses Analytics.
- Comparison page lists Elite at $249.99 (down from prior $299) — possible price cut. Verify on next run.
- Still the fastest feature shipper in the market. SEO content machine running across 10+ verticals.

### New Entrants to Watch
- **Servinix** — Beta Aug 17 with "60% off invoice" switcher incentive. Launch day. Must monitor.
- **Shifton** — Free tier up to 10 employees. Directly competes with MowGo Free.
- **GreenRoute** — Free core + $10/mo route optimization. Cheapest paid option.
- **FieldCamp** — AI dispatching, custom reporting. Mid-market threat.
