**The wedge that survives:** Grassly charges a % of every payment on top (2% free / 1.25% starter / 0.5% pro) **plus** Stripe's own fees. MowGo's flat $39/$79 with standard Stripe rates = "pay us once, keep 100% of every dollar." That's the honest, verifiable counter-line vs fee-on-top freemium. Also Grassly Free caps at 100 customers / 1 user → "grow past free" story.

**Pricing decision impact (Day 7):** A MowGo free tier would now be table-stakes parity, not a differentiator. Recommendation: hold flat pricing, market "no % processing fees," and let Grassly's 2%-on-free do the selling. Revisit only if trial starts stall.
