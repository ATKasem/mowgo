### 4. 🟡 SIGNIFICANT: GreenRoute Pricing Correction
War Chest says: "no reviews, free tier stripped of everything useful, client portal locked at $199, rain NOT auto-rescheduled (forecast only)"
ACTUAL pricing (from greenrouteapp.com/Pricing):

| Tier | Price | Key Features |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Starter | $0/mo | Unlimited customers/contracts/invoices, satellite measurement, mobile app (iOS+Android), 5 photos/record |
| Professional | $10/mo | Adds route planning, weather forecasts on jobs, service/payment reminders, SMS, unlimited photos, revenue dashboards |
