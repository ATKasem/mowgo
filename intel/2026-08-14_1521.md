### Feature Ideas Research Findings

#### 1. Route Optimization — #1 Missing Feature Across All Plans

**Status:** Every competitor from $0 to $699/mo has some form of route optimization. MowGo does not.

**Competitor implementations:**
- **QuoteIQ:** Built-in on higher plans, aerial lot measurement included
- **Jobber:** Built-in on Connect ($119+) with route optimization + client portal
- **Service Autopilot:** Smart Maps add-on for routing
- **RealGreen:** Dynamic routing auto-adds jobs to daily routes
- **LawnPro:** AI route optimization on Startup ($39+)
- **ProBase:** Free, built-in
- **GreenRoute:** $10/mo add-on for route optimization
- **Yardbook:** Basic routing on free tier

**Why this matters:** Route density is the single biggest margin lever in lawn care. A crew losing 40 min/day to bad routing burns ~$565/month in loaded labor. This isn't a nice-to-have — it's table stakes.

**Build difficulty:** Medium. Need geocoding (Google Maps/Mapbox API) + simple nearest-neighbor heuristic. Can start with basic optimization (sort by proximity) and iterate to TSP-like solutions.

#### 2. Level Billing / Drought Adjustment — Top User Request

**What it is:** Automatically adjust invoices when weather impacts service delivery (rain missed weeks, drought-shortened mowing cycles).

**Competitors with it:** LawnPro (FREE), Jobber (partial via automations)

**MowGo status:** NOT built

**Why it matters NOW:** Oklahoma drought continues into August. Customers mowing every 10 days instead of 7 want fair billing. This is a churn risk if not addressed.

**Business value:** Reduces customer complaints, reduces manual invoice adjustments, positions MowGo as customer-friendly. Rain Delay v2 + Level Billing = killer combo for summer.

#### 3. Marketplace Integration Option — ProBase Model

**What ProBase proved:** Operators will accept "free software in exchange for optional marketplace exposure." ProBase takes a percentage of marketplace-sourced jobs only. You opt out completely and keep everything free.

**Opportunity for MowGo:** Add an *optional* lead-sharing feature where business owners can opt into a local leads network. Not a marketplace requirement — just an opt-in referral network between MowGo businesses. Differentiators vs. ProBase: no ecosystem lock-in, complete data ownership, no pressure to accept jobs.

**Risk:** Low. Purely opt-in. Could drive organic growth through cross-referrals.

#### 4. Offline Mode Enhancement — Android Priority

**Current state:** iOS has documented offline mode. Android native app needs offline parity.

**Key gap:** Android users lose access to schedules and invoices without connectivity. Solo operators working rural routes (common in OK/TX markets) regularly face spotty signal.

**Feature spec:** Cache jobs/schedules/customer data locally. Sync when connection restored. Conflict resolution for edits made offline.

#### 5. Weather Intelligence Expansion — Beyond Rain Delay

**What exists:** Open-Meteo rain probability → banner on Today screen. User can reschedule manually.

**What's missing:**
- Wind advisory alerts (blows mulch, ruins blow-off work)
- Heat index warnings (crew safety — OSHA guidelines trigger at certain thresholds)
- Humidity forecasts (fungicide/disease spray windows)
- Frost alerts (spring/fall season extensions)

**Build difficulty:** Easy. Open-Meteo already provides all these APIs. Just surface them in the UI.

#### 6. Payment Plan / Layaway Option

**Market signal:** Facebook group post (Aug 14) shows lawn care services sending "monthly billing" contracts with late fees after 3 days. Small operators are doing this manually because software doesn't support it.

**What it is:** Let recurring clients choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly payment cadences. Not traditional layaway — just flexible billing timing.

**Competitors:** Most handle this as part of recurring billing. MowGo's invoicing currently assumes standard recurring intervals.

**Value proposition:** Customer retention. Easier for cash-strapped solo operators to manage cash flow.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Route optimization is non-negotiable.** Every free and paid alternative has it. Without it, MowGo looks behind-the-curve to any buyer comparing options. Build priority: HIGH.

2. **Level billing is urgent for drought season.** Oklahoma lawns are suffering. Customers who normally mow weekly are now going 10-14 days and resent paying full price. Rain Delay handles the scheduling; Level Billing handles the billing. Together they solve the "what do I owe you when it rained all week?" problem.

3. **Servinix beta hits Monday (Aug 17).** Sign up. They're promising crew GPS + rain delay + unlimited crews at an aggressive price. Need first-hand evaluation within 48 hours.

4. **ProBase confirms the free-software threat is real.** Their marketing is sharp ("Sunday night problem," "7am route problem"), their feature set is complete, and they have zero cost to switch from. MowGo's defense: better UX, native apps, privacy (your data stays yours, not fed into a marketplace).

5. **FieldVibe enters the free tier space** with native iOS/Android apps, 4.8-star ratings, and genuine free tier. They lack payments/invoicing but their mobile polish could steal users from the Free tier who don't need those features yet.
