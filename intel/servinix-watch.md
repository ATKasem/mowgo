## Threat Assessment
- **Direct threat at 2+ crew ICP** — Servinix targets commercial (1-100 techs), MowGo's Crew ICP is 2-5 person operations
- **Rain delay automation overlap** — MowGo already ships this; the edge is one-button simplicity + no onboarding
- **Invoice switcher hook** — "60% off your invoice" is the first time a competitor has used invoice-based pricing to bait switchers. Watch for pricing transparency at beta launch
- **Pricing unknown at beta** — if Servinix reveals beta pricing, add to competitor-comparison.md immediately
