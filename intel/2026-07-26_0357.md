- "No trial period — free means free."

- Quotes & invoices
- Route optimization
- Time tracking & payroll

- Receipt snaps & expenses
- Reports, margins & exports (P&L, job margins)
- Text & email reminders (automatic)
- Mileage tracker
- Payment acceptance: cards, ACH, Venmo, PayPal, Cash App, Zelle
- AI assistant "Winston" (support-focused, not conversational CRM Autopilot)
- Review requests (Google, Yelp, Facebook)

- Team management with permissions

- CRM & follow-up sequences
- Business phone & recording
- Online booking page
- Free business website
- Mail paper invoices (!)

### Weaknesses Discovered

2. **No rain delay at any tier** — critical gap vs MowGo
3. 3.5% transaction fee is higher than MowGo's transparent pricing model
4. "Winston" AI is template/FAQ support, NOT conversational CRM Autopilot
5. No chemical/application tracking
6. Newer/unproven compared to Yardbook (since 2013)
7. No offline mode confirmed
8. Self-published comparison blog positions DoorstepHQ as #1 free option — they're trying to own the "free lawn care software" SEO space
