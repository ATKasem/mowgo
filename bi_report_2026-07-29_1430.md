**Source:** Adeocode detailed breakdown (verified Jul 25, 2026). Jobber moved from flat plans to team-size configs without announcement.

| Plan | Configs | Annual (mo) | Monthly | Add-on users |
|------|---------|:-----------:|:-------:|:------------:|
| Core | 1 user | $21/mo | $49/mo | +$29/mo |
| Connect | 1/5/10/15 users | $70–$210/mo | $139–$399/mo | +$29/mo |
| Grow | 1/5/10/15 users | $105–$280/mo | $199–$499/mo | +$29/mo |
| Plus | 5/10/15 users | $280–$371/mo | $499–$699/mo | +$29/mo |

**Critical details:**
- Annual rates shown are **first-year pricing** — renewals are higher (15-user Plus: $529/mo renewal)
- There's a hidden middle option: monthly billing with 1-year commitment (e.g. Core $39/mo)
- Add-on stack is where the cost balloons:
  - Marketing Suite: +$79/mo
  - AI Receptionist: +$29/mo
  - Pipeline: +$49/mo
  - **Solo Grow + Marketing + AI = $213/mo** — add-ons cost more than the base plan
- $29/mo overage fee applies to EVERY extra user on EVERY plan
- API access gated behind Plus ($280–371/mo)
