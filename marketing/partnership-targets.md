## The Hormozi affiliate structure (when launching Tier 2 creator deals)

From digest §2326-2333:
1. **Two-tier rewards:** Top 10 affiliates get an exclusive Q&A + dinner. Top 50 get a 3-hour session. Everyone with 10+ signups gets a bonus. The prize must benefit BOTH the affiliate and their audience.
2. **Plug-and-play collateral:** Pre-written emails, posts, ads, and test messages affiliates can copy/paste. "If you expect them to make an entire campaign to launch your thing, good luck."
3. **Real-time leaderboard:** Competitors want to check daily. Send frequently; personal outreach to top performers.
4. **Behind-the-scenes access:** Share which emails/ads are converting. One of the most underrated incentives — affiliates learn marketing by watching yours.
5. **Promotion channels:** Email list (max 2x), email last year's affiliates, link on thank-you page, free content explaining the program.
