## Send order

- Send one message at a time.
- Personalize the first sentence to the business and city.
- Ask the workflow question.
- Do not attach a generic pitch or claim that MowGo will increase revenue.
- Log the result immediately in `leads/LEAD_TRACKER.md`.

## Reply handling

- **They answer with a workflow:** ask the next qualification question; then offer the pilot.
- **They ask for the app:** send the production link and offer to set up the first week personally; explain that concierge submission is available after Solo, Crew, or Premium trial/plan activation.
- **They ask for price:** say Solo is $39/month after the pilot; do not negotiate before they experience the workflow.
- **They say they already use Jobber/Yardbook:** ask what still requires manual work. Do not attack their current tool.
- **No response:** one respectful follow-up, then stop.
- **They decline:** record the reason; do not argue.

## Founder-only actions still required

Outreach is the main remaining work, but it is not literally the only work. For every pilot, Aaron must:

1. help a qualified prospect activate a Solo, Crew, or Premium trial/plan;
2. monitor the concierge queue and import the client list;
3. schedule the first week, review skipped rows, and mark the request done;
4. verify the imported data and first operating week with the operator;
5. observe real usage;
6. capture the first measurable win;
7. ask for payment;
8. request testimonial permission only after a genuine win.

No automation or fabricated proof substitutes for these steps at the first-10 stage.

## Definition of outreach-ready

- The message has a named recipient and specific context.
- The current workflow is unknown, not assumed.
- The offer is a pilot, not an unsupported promise.
- The next step is one clear question.
- The tracker is updated after sending.
- Any result is recorded only after it happens.
