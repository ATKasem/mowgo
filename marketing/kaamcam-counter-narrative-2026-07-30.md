# KaamCam Counter-Narrative
**Drafted:** 2026-07-30
**Context:** KaamCam ($12/seat) publishing articles arguing solo ops don't need full features. Need growth-scalability messaging.

---

## Positioning Strategy

**Don't attack KaamCam on price.** They're $12/seat — we're $39 flat. The argument isn't "we're cheaper" (we're not for a single user). The argument is **"you'll outgrow them in 3 months."**

### Core Narrative

**"Software for where you're going, not where you are."**

KaamCam is optimized for the solo operator who plans to stay solo — cheap per-seat, but structured for individuals. MowGo is designed for the operator who wants to grow: one flat price regardless of crew size, so adding your first hire doesn't double your software bill.

### Key Message Pillars

#### 1. The Growth Ceiling
- **KaamCam:** $12/seat. First hire = $24/mo. Second hire = $36/mo. Third hire = $48/mo. Fourth hire = $60/mo. Scales linearly with every person you add.
- **MowGo:** $39 flat. First hire = $39. Fifth hire = $39. Tenth hire = $39. Adding crew members costs $0 in software.
- **Message:** "Don't pay per person. Your crew grows your revenue — your software should grow with it, not tax it."

#### 2. Rain Delay ≠ Scheduler
- **KaamCam:** Positions rain delay as a solo-op feature. Single-day rescheduling only.
- **MowGo:** Rain delay + full recurring route scheduling + crew assignment. One tap moves an entire day's jobs. Route optimization prevents "mow the same street twice" — the cost of a missed optimization is higher than any seat price difference.

#### 3. Feature Depth for Growth
- **KaamCam:** Best for "just me, just mowing, just today."
- **MowGo:** Multi-service (lawn + landscaping + holiday lights + snow), client portal, auto-invoicing, payment processing, job scheduling, recurring routes, rain delay.
- **Message:** "When your business grows from 'just me' to 'me + 2 guys,' will your software grow with you?"

#### 4. The Real Cost Story
- **Single operator comparing:** KaamCam $12 vs MowGo $39 — yes, MowGo costs more for one person.
- **But:** If you hire one crew member, KaamCam becomes $24/mo (+100% increase). MowGo stays $39.
- **At 3 crew:** KaamCam = $48. MowGo = $39. MowGo is now cheaper.
- **At 5 crew:** KaamCam = $72. MowGo = $39. MowGo is 46% cheaper.
- **Message:** "Buy your software for the team you want, not just the size you are today."
