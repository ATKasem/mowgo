## The script

### 1. Promise (30 sec) — say this, don't read it
> "Hey [NAME], thanks for the 15 minutes. By the end of this call you'll know exactly whether [PRODUCT] can save you [NUMBER] hours a week — and if it can, I'll have it set up for you by [DAY OF WEEK]."

**Fill in:**
- [NAME]: _______________
- [PRODUCT]: _______________
- [NUMBER]: _______________ (estimate from crew size: 1-2 crew = 5-8 hrs/wk on scheduling/admin)
- [DAY OF WEEK]: _______________

### 2. Story (60 sec) — the honesty beat
> "Quick story — I built this because I ran a lawn crew and kept losing jobs to missed texts. [ONE CONCRETE PAIN STORY: specific lost client, double-booked day, rain mess]. So I built [PRODUCT] to fix that exact problem. [ONE LINE: what it does — scheduling, rain delays, auto-invoicing]."

**Fill in your story (write it BEFORE the call):**
- The lost job: ___________________________________________
- The double-book: ________________________________________
- The rain mess: __________________________________________

### 3. Three concerns (3 min) — ask, don't assume
> "Before I tell you how it works, three quick questions — I want to make sure it actually fits your operation."

1. **Rain:** "What happens now when it rains mid-week?" → their answer → *"That's the #1 thing we fix. You pick the new day, we move every job + text the client automatically."* (Remember: suggestion-only, never auto — locked decision.)
2. **Scheduling:** "How do you remember who's weekly vs biweekly?" → *"MowGo holds every route, so you see the week at a glance and never miss a cut."*
3. **Money:** "What do you do for invoices?" → *"Auto-invoice after each job. You get paid without the awkward 'hey, just checking on that invoice' text."*

### 4. The offer (2 min)
> "Here's what I'll do this week: I'll migrate your current clients in, build your routes, set up your rain-delay flow, and send your first texts with you watching. [30 days free, no card]. If you don't love it, you keep everything we set up and walk away."

**Offer elements — fill in:**
- Free period: [30] days
- What you'll do for them (migrate, routes, rain flow, first texts): _______________
- Their only job: [approve / watch / confirm]

### 5. Live FAQ (3 min) — from real buckets
Anticipate + answer from the concierge request data:
- "Is it hard to learn?" → *"That's the point — I set it up, you just approve."*
- "What if I already have a system?" → *"Then this is a 30-day free trial of something better. No risk."*
- "How is this different from [Jobber/ServiceTitan]?" → *"Built for a 1-3 person crew, priced for you, and I set it up personally."*
- "What does it cost after?" → *"[SOLO $39 / CREW $79] a month — and the annual plan is [16% off]."*

## The conversion ask (Day 30 — at the win, not before)
> "You're saving [X] hours a week. Want to keep it going at [PRICE]? Most operators pick the annual plan — one decision, done for the year, [16%] off."

**Annual-first — present annual as the default option.** Never discount monthly; change the structure (annual/quarterly) or add value instead.

---

## If they stall — step-down (never discount)
- Offer quarterly billing instead of monthly.
- Add a free first month with a testimonial commitment.
- Add a referral credit for bringing another lawn company.
