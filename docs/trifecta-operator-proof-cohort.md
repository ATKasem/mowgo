# MowGo Trifecta Operator Proof Cohort

## Purpose
Prove the shipped Rain-Proof Launch and concierge workflow with 5–10 real lawn operators before paid acquisition or stronger outcome claims.

## Cohort workflow

1. Recruit an operator in the target ICP: solo or 2–3-person crew, roughly 5–30 recurring clients, spreadsheet/pen-and-paper or switching from a simpler tool.
2. Record consent to participate and the starting workflow: current client count, weekly jobs, scheduling method, invoicing method, and biggest breakage.
3. Create/sign in to the account and record signup time.
4. Verify the operator reaches activation: first client, first job, first invoice.
5. For paid-tier participants, submit concierge CSV and record request time, tier, raw rows, cleaned rows, duplicates, and setup status.
6. Operator reviews the first operating week. Record corrections, missing clients, duplicate matches, and schedule changes.
7. Record the first invoice sent and the first rain-delay or reschedule event when it naturally occurs. Do not manufacture a weather claim.
8. Ask for feedback after a real operating win. Request a testimonial only with permission and only using verified facts.

## Required metrics

- Signup → first client: hours
- Signup → first job: hours

- Concierge submit → import complete: hours
- Concierge submit → first-week preparation: hours
- Raw rows / cleaned rows / duplicate rows / rejected rows

- Trial → paid conversion

## Operator interview prompts

- What did you use before MowGo?
- What nearly caused you to miss or double-book work?
- What was the first moment MowGo saved time or prevented a mistake?
- What did you have to correct in the imported schedule?
- What would make you keep using it next season?
- What would make Premium worth $199/month?

## Evidence rules

- No invented savings, revenue, time, or weather outcomes.
- A case study must identify the operator's starting state, observed workflow change, time window, and permission to publish.
- Do not use one operator's result as a universal claim.
- Keep raw notes private; publish only approved, anonymized or explicitly attributed evidence.

## Decision rules after 5–10 operators

- **Continue:** at least 70% reach first client + first job + first invoice within 7 days, no unresolved concierge data-integrity issue, and at least one operator gives permission for a case study.
- **Fix before acquisition:** any client/job loss, duplicate billing, incorrect schedule, or repeated concierge failure.
- **Hold Premium expansion:** if operators cannot explain why priority queue placement matters or if the operator process still depends on Aaron improvising each setup.
- **Start paid acquisition:** only after activation and proof are measured for the cohort and the landing/offer copy matches those observed results.

## Founder leverage checklist

- Aaron runs the first 5–10 setups.
- Every exception becomes a checklist item or template.
- After five setups, separate work into Shadow → Supervise → Support.
- Track minutes per setup and the percentage requiring Aaron's direct intervention.
- Do not promise a faster SLA until measured capacity supports it.
