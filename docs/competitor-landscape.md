## Platform by Platform

### QuoteIQ — #1 threat
- **Price:** $29.99-$699/mo, no per-user fees
- **Strengths:** Route optimization + MapMeasure Pro aerial measurement + InstaQuote self-service quoting. Mike Vidan (580K YouTube) + Justin Rogers (743K YouTube) drive massive free distribution. Published pricing, no sales call needed.
- **Weaknesses:** No rain delay, no offline mode, no chemical tracking. Newer platform (founded 2022).
- **MowGo edge:** Rain delay, offline mode, lower entry price (free tier). But QuoteIQ outguns you on feature depth at $29.99.

### Jobber — Generalist safe choice
- **Price:** $49-$499/mo, $29/user after 1 user
- **Strengths:** Polished UX, 20K+ user community, easy learning curve. Best client portal in the space.
- **Weaknesses:** Per-user fees kill scaling. No lawn-specific features. Route optimization gated to mid-tier.
- **MowGo edge:** 3-5x cheaper at scale, lawn-specific features, no per-user fees.

### RealGreen (WorkWave) — Enterprise chemical focus
- **Price:** ~$199/mo custom (quote-gated)
- **Strengths:** 40 years of green-industry depth. Dynamic Routing built for chemical treatment. Compliance-grade tracking. Scales to 200+ crews.
- **Weaknesses:** More platform than solo operators need. Implementation is a project. Pricing hidden behind sales call.
- **MowGo edge:** 5x cheaper, instant setup, no sales call. Not competing for the same customer (treatment vs mowing).

### LawnPro — Tiny operator starter
- **Price:** Free (50 clients) / $39/mo
- **Strengths:** Free tier. Recurring contract billing. Simple.
- **Weaknesses:** No route optimization. No mobile app quality. Poor support.
- **MowGo edge:** Route optimization, mobile apps, rain delay, offline mode. Same price point for Solo.

### Bodha — Emerging scheduling+routing
- **Price:** Free+ (not fully published)
- **Strengths:** Scheduling + routing in one. Client notes + gate codes. Adherence reporting.
- **Weaknesses:** No invoicing (not a full suite). Newer platform. Limited feature set.
- **MowGo edge:** Full suite (scheduling+routing+invoicing), rain delay, offline mode.

## MowGo's Position

### True differentiators (nobody else has):
1. **Rain delay one-tap move** — Oklahoma spring storms? One tap moves every job to the next dry day. Clients notified automatically. No phone calls.
2. **Offline mode** — Works without cell service in rural areas. Syncs when back in range.
3. **No per-user fees on any plan** — $39 flat, $79 flat, $199 flat. Competitors charge $29/user (Jobber).

### Table stakes (have but not unique):
- Route optimization (nearest-neighbor + 2-opt)
- Job scheduling
- Recurring billing
- Mobile apps
- Client notes

### Missing (competitors have):
- Aerial property measurement (QuoteIQ)
- Chemical treatment compliance (RealGreen)
- Client self-service portal (Jobber, QuoteIQ)
- Customer online booking (QuoteIQ, Jobber, Housecall Pro)

**Recommendation:**
- Lead with rain delay in every piece of marketing. It's the only feature nobody else has.
- Add aerial property measurement (MapMeasure equivalent) to close the gap with QuoteIQ.
- Raise Solo price to $49/mo (still cheaper than Jobber, same as Yardbook, matches perceived value of route optimization).
- Build the "Free Route Audit" into a full diagnostic that integrates with the product — not just an email.
