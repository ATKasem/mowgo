APP STORE REVIEW NOTE — MowGo (com.mowgo.app)

Copy this into App Store Connect → App Store tab → App Review Information → Notes:

---
MowGo is business management software for lawn care and landscaping service companies
(scheduling, route management, rain-delay rescheduling, and invoicing). It is a B2B
field-service tool sold to business owners — the paid plans unlock business workflow
features used outside the app (scheduling operations, invoicing, crew management).

Subscriptions are processed through Stripe Checkout (opened only on checkout.stripe.com)
rather than StoreKit IAP because MowGo is a multi-platform business application: the same
subscription must work seamlessly across web, iOS, and Android, and the service itself is
delivered as a business tool, not digital content consumed within the app. This follows
the established precedent for business/enterprise apps that manage real-world services
(e.g., scheduling, field-service, and operations software).

No digital content is sold or consumed inside the app. The app contains no store, no
media, no consumables. Payments made through the app are for the business software
subscription itself.
