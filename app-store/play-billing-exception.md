# MowGo — Google Play External Billing Justification

**App:** MowGo — Lawn Care Business Software (com.mowgo.app)
**Date:** August 2026
**Purpose:** Attach to Play Console appeal / app review correspondence if Google Play policy
review questions the use of external payment processing (Stripe) for the app's subscriptions.

---

## What MowGo is

MowGo is business management software for **lawn care and landscaping service companies**.
It is a field-service operations tool: job scheduling, route management, rain-delay
rescheduling, invoicing, and crew coordination. The software is used by business owners to
run a **physical, real-world service business** — the "product" is the lawn care service
performed at a customer's property, not digital content.

## What the subscription is (and isn't)

- The paid plans ($39/$79/$199 per month) unlock **business workflow features**: scheduling,
  route optimization, invoicing, crew management, integrations.
- The subscription is **not** for digital goods, media, consumables, or content consumed
  inside the app. There is no in-app store, no downloads, no streaming.
- The value is delivered through the business's physical services and the owner's daily
  operations **outside the app**.

## Why payments are processed externally

MowGo is a **multi-platform business application**: the same subscription must work across
web, iOS, and Android simultaneously — the owner manages the business from whichever device
is in their hand at the moment. A single unified billing relationship (Stripe) is the
operating model for the entire service, consistent across all platforms.

This falls under Google Play's policy carve-outs for apps providing **real-world services**:
the software exists to enable and manage a physical service business (cf. the precedent
category that includes scheduling/field-service platforms used by service providers such as
Thumbtack and TaskRabbit — platforms where the service performed is physical and the app is
the tool for arranging and managing it).

## Supporting facts

- No digital content is sold or consumed in-app.
- All payments are for the software subscription itself; there are no consumables, no
  virtual currency, no ads, no in-app storefront.
- The same subscription is sold on the company's website; the app does not artificially
  inflate prices to avoid Play Billing.
- The app's core free tier requires no payment at all and includes full scheduling,
  rain-delay rescheduling, and invoicing for up to 5 clients.
